Put your client branding files in this folder:

  logo.png        ← App logo (shown in sidebar, login page, PWA icon area)
                    Recommended: square, min 200×200px, transparent background
                    Supported formats: .png .jpg .svg .webp

  login-bg.jpg    ← Login page left-panel background image
                    Recommended: landscape photo, min 1200×800px, dark/moody tone

After adding files here, update frontend/src/lib/branding.config.ts:

  logoPath:     '/branding/logo.png'
  loginBgPath:  '/branding/login-bg.jpg'

Then rebuild:
  docker-compose up -d --build
